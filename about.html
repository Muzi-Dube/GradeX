About GradeX

What is GradeX?

GradeX is a simple grade calculator tool that aims to help you get feedback on your grade average, GPA, & APS score, as well as a statistics on your overall performance.

Why GradeX?

The purpose of this website application is simply to help you get an overview of your academic performance, while at the same time automating calculations that would need to be done manually if otherwise. This saves you time and effort, allowing you to focus more on self accessment and improvement.

Future versions of GradeX

Future versions of GradeX will surely be better than each last update. We hope to add more powerful features to enhance your experiences with GradeX. We can't wait to redefine the future of academia together with you, our community.

features of GradeX (version.1.0)

  • Grade average calculations
  • GPA(4.0) calculations
  • APS score calculations
  • Grade average & GPA stats-bar
  • Real-time graph representations
