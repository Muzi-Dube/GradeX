Help & FAQ

How do I calculate my final grade?

Enter each assignment/exam score and its weight in the calculator. The tool multiplies each score by its weight and sums the results to give your final percentage.

Can I save my data?

This version does not persist data remotely. You can copy results or use browser tools to save locally.

What grading scale is used?

The calculator uses percentages. You can map percentages to letter grades manually or update your local copy of styles/scripts to include a scale conversion.

Where can I report bugs?

Use the Contacts page to submit bug reports or suggestions.
